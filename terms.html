Using AIDC

You may use AIDC to create and manage identity applications and their OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect configuration when you have the necessary authorization. You are responsible for the applications, domains, redirect URIs, credentials, and integrations you configure.

Account security

Keep your Ace ID account secure and do not share credentials, client secrets, recovery information, or other authentication material. Notify the service team promptly if you believe an account or application credential has been compromised.

Application configuration

You are responsible for ensuring that configured origins, redirect URIs, scopes, branding, and application behavior are accurate and lawful. DNS verification is a security control, not a guarantee that a configured application is safe or trustworthy.
