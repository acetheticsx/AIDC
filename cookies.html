Cookie Policy

The cookies AIDC uses for authentication, OAuth security, and essential console functionality.

Last updated October 4, 2026

What cookies AIDC uses

AIDC uses a small number of cookies required for authentication and request security. These include a session cookie, a CSRF protection cookie, and short-lived OAuth state and PKCE verifier cookies used during the sign-in flow.

Authentication session

The AIDC session cookie identifies an authenticated developer session. It is used to keep you signed in to the developer console and is not intended as an advertising or cross-site tracking identifier.

Security cookies

The CSRF cookie supports protection for state-changing requests. During Ace ID sign-in, temporary state and PKCE verifier cookies help bind the authorization response to the sign-in attempt. These OAuth cookies are short-lived and scoped to the authentication path.

Third-party services

AIDC may load required developer-facing resources from third-party infrastructure. Browser requests to those providers may be subject to their own privacy practices. AIDC does not use those resources to intentionally create an advertising profile.

Cookie controls

Blocking or clearing required cookies can prevent authentication or other secure console functions from working correctly. You can control cookies through your browser, but disabling essential cookies may require you to sign in again.

Updates

This policy may change when authentication, security, or supporting infrastructure changes. The date above identifies the current published version.
